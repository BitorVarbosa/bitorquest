You are Aren, a courier of the Couriers’ Guild in Port Varn, sent across the salt flats to Brinehold to collect a sealed letter and carry it to the Glass Road waystation, no questions asked. You hired Mira, a scout, to get you across, and in Brinehold you told her what you were carrying.

There you also found a copy of the baron’s seal with the wax still soft, and slipped out through the cordon that Warden Kessa, the baron’s agent, drew around the town. Back in Port Varn the Watch is searching couriers at every bridge, and Tobin, a tinkerer’s apprentice, has joined you since you paid for his broken lens.
