How bonds work

A bond runs from 0 to 100. It measures trust and shared history: in practice, how much someone would do for you. Every 20 points is a stage.

  • Acquaintance 1–19Remembers you, talks freely, helps for a price
  • Friendly 20–39Does favours, passes on what they hear
  • Trusted 40–59Joins the party, talks about their past
  • Close 60–79Opens a personal quest, helps in tests, confides secrets
  • Devoted 80–100Takes serious risks, acts as one with you in a crisis

Most of a bond comes from a few acts that mattered. Small courtesies add a little and are capped each day.

A new stage brings a moment. A scene with that person, played when you spend time together.

Attitude is separate. It is how someone feels about you right now, and it can change within a scene. A bond cannot.

There is no negative bond. An enemy has no bond, a hostile attitude and a grievance.
