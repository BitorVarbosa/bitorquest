The whole story

Aren, a courier of the Couriers’ Guild in Port Varn, crossed the salt flats to Brinehold for a sealed letter bound for the Glass Road waystation, no questions asked. He came away with the letter and a secret: someone is copying the baron’s seal. Hunted by the baron’s agents, he is back in Port Varn with Mira, a scout, and Tobin, a tinkerer’s apprentice, and has just bluffed his way past Captain Roth of the City Watch, who carries a copy himself.

The Sealed Letter

Days 1–8 · 78 turns

Guildmaster Hask sent Aren across the salt flats to Brinehold for a sealed letter, to be carried to the Glass Road waystation with no questions asked. At the North Gate he hired Mira, a scout, and in Brinehold he took the letter, told her what he was really carrying, and found a copy of the baron’s seal with the wax still soft. When Warden Kessa and the baron’s agents cordoned the town, he slipped out.
