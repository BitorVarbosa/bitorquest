Who do you play?

One or two sentences. You build the character itself after the world.

This decides where detail is spent. The places, people and trouble closest to your character are built first and in most depth.

Leave it empty and you get suggestions that fit the world when you create the character.

  • Where the story startsA courier starts in a Guild house, so that town is built in full.
  • Who you meet firstGuild masters, gate guards and rival couriers get names and goals.

The rest of the world stays a sketch until a story goes there, so you never pay for places you do not visit.

The world

Canal cities and a road of glass across the salt flats. Couriers, forged seals, and a baron who is losing his grip.

9 regions · 14 places · 23 people

Regions · 9

  • Port Varn The story starts here

    A canal city of bridges, guild houses and lantern markets.

  • The Salt Flats

    White, trackless ground between the coast and Brinehold. Days to cross.

  • Brinehold

    A salt-mining town on the far side of the flats, watched by the baron’s wardens.

  • The Glass Road

    A road of fused sand running east across the flats.

  • Tessaly

    The baron’s seat, upriver.

  • Greywater Marsh

    Reed channels and stilt villages, home of the Marsh-born.

  • The Lantern Isles

    A chain of lighthouse islands that guide ships in to the coast.

  • The Chalk Hills

    Sheep country and old quarries above the flats.

  • The Ember Shore

    Black sand and glassmakers’ kilns that are never let go cold.
